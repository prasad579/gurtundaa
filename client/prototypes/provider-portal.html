Milk & Laundry Provider Portal — Prototype

New read-only portal so a milkman/dhobi can register their own account, link the households who already added them as a contact, and see an aggregated day-to-day view of who gave them what — without the household doing anything differently. Linking happens via a one-time invite code shared from the household's contact card (no phone-number auto-matching, no account conflicts). The household stays the single source of truth — quantities, pickups and "paid" status are all set from their side and simply mirrored here.
