Milk 🥛 — Simplified Redesign — Prototype

The current screen works but front-loads everything at once: a contact card with a 4-stat grid, two separate "today" cards, a separate pause card, a calendar + 4 clickable stat buttons + 3 rows of amounts + payment status + payments list + 2 share buttons all in one card, a horizontal "upcoming schedule" scroller mixing 3 different item types, and an always-expanded activity feed. Nothing is wrong individually — it's just a lot to scan before you can answer "did milk come today, and do I owe anything?" This prototype keeps every feature (nothing removed) but re-prioritizes by how often you actually need it.

  • One hero card for "today" (was 2 separate cards) — tap the ✅/❌ circle itself to toggle status; quantity and overflow (notify / edit) tucked behind a "⋯" instead of 3 buttons always visible.
  • Pause state replaces the today-cards entirely with one clear banner + a single gradient "Resume" button — instead of muted placeholder text in each slot card plus a whole separate pause card below.
  • Bill is one big number ("₹840 due") with a "see breakdown ›" link, not 3 always-open amount rows + a payments ledger always visible.
  • 3 big quick-action tiles (Extra / Pause / Change Qty) replace the "+" action-sheet — same 3 actions, zero taps to discover them.
  • "Upcoming changes" and "Recent activity" become single collapsed links with a count badge, not always-rendered scrollers/lists — tap to expand only when you care.
  • Tapping a calendar day opens a proper bottom sheet (not a native stacked-button alert) with the quantity/pause actions as a clean 2×2 pill grid.
  • Brand gradient (#7c3aed → #ec4899 → #f97316) now actually shows up — on the hero header and primary CTA — instead of flat purple everywhere.
